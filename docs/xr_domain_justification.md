# XR Domain Justification: Mobile WebXR + Tabletop Holographic Kiosk vs. Enclosed HMD VR

Deploying heritage spatial computing across open-air coastal monuments in South India (**Group of Monuments at Mahabalipuram**, `12.6165° N, 80.19° E`) imposes heat, hygiene, throughput, and safety constraints where enclosed Head-Mounted Displays (VR/MR headsets) are poorly suited.

| Constraint Dimension | Enclosed VR/MR Headset (Rejected Outdoors) | Lunar Aurora Hybrid Prototype |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solar Thermal Load** | Sealed facial gaskets trap heat and sweat outdoors in direct tropical sun; external tracking cameras risk thermal throttling. | Visitors use personal smartphones in brief bursts. The FastAPI backend pairs NOAA solar geometry (`12.6165° N`) with graph edge shade weights (`tree_avenue`, `cave_approach`, `cave_shelter`) to recommend lower-exposure routes (**`80.5%` model estimate** on `2027-01-11 12:00 IST` from Stop 1 to Stop 7 — calculated model index, not a physical dosimeter reading). |
| **Tourist Hygiene & Queue Throughput** | Shared public headsets require disinfection between visitors at high-footfall ticket counters. | QR-launched browser WebXR (`BYOD`, zero app install) avoids shared headwear; the tabletop kiosk at the visitor entrance requires no headset (`No-SRD simulation mode` on standard monitors today, with Sony ELF-SR1 glasses-free lenticular integration documented in `docs/sony_srd_integration_todo.md` as pending SDK install). |
| **Situational & Monument Safety** | Full-face enclosures narrow peripheral vision around unguarded bedrock slopes and ancient stone steps. | Camera-first Three.js WebXR leaves peripheral sightlines clear while walking; `DeviceOrientationEvent`-coupled WebAudio (`spatialAudio.js`) delivers spatialized EN/TA/HI narration without requiring continuous screen staring. |
| **Shared Group Viewing at Visitor Center** | Placing an elderly visitor or child inside an HMD isolates them from companions. | The Unity 6 desktop kiosk displays procedural illustrative architectural layers + operator telemetry to family groups simultaneously on standard monitors (`Operator HUD (second display)` on dual monitors; ready for future Sony ELF-SR1 SDK integration). |
