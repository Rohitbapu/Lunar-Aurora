# XR Domain Justification: Mobile WebXR + Tabletop Holographic Kiosk vs. Enclosed HMD VR

Deploying spatial computing across open-air coastal heritage sites in South India (**Group of Monuments at Mahabalipuram**, `12.6165° N, 80.19° E`) imposes solar heat, hygiene, throughput, and situational awareness constraints where enclosed Head-Mounted Displays (VR/MR headsets) are poorly suited.

| Constraint Dimension | Enclosed VR/MR Headset (Rejected Outdoors) | Lunar Aurora Hybrid Prototype |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solar Thermal Load** | Sealed facial gaskets trap heat and sweat outdoors in direct tropical sun; external tracking cameras risk thermal throttling. | Visitors use personal smartphones in short scan/read bursts. The FastAPI routing engine combines real OpenStreetMap distances with NOAA solar elevation/azimuth (`12.6165° N, 80.19° E`) and **assumed/illustrative** canopy weights (`81.4%` illustrative score on `2027-01-11 12:00 IST` — not measured sun exposure) to highlight shadier routes pending physical calibration. |
| **Tourist Hygiene & Queue Throughput** | Shared public headsets require disinfection between visitors at high-footfall ticket counters. | QR-launched browser WebXR (`BYOD`, zero install) avoids shared headwear; the visitor-center Unity 6 desktop kiosk requires no headset (`No-SRD simulation mode` on standard monitors today, with future Sony ELF-SR1 integration documented in `docs/sony_srd_integration_todo.md`). |
| **Situational & Monument Safety** | Full-face enclosures narrow peripheral vision around unguarded granite slopes and ancient stone steps. | Camera-first Three.js WebXR leaves peripheral sightlines clear while walking between stops (`~167 m` to `~1831 m` apart via OSM footpaths); `DeviceOrientationEvent`-coupled WebAudio (`spatialAudio.js`) delivers EN/TA/HI audio hands-free. |
| **Shared Group Viewing at Visitor Center** | Placing an elderly visitor or child inside an HMD isolates them from companions. | The Unity 6 desktop kiosk displays procedural illustrative architectural layers + operator telemetry to family groups simultaneously (`3840x2160` fullscreen by default, `-windowed` CLI switch for development, plus `Operator HUD (second display)`). |
