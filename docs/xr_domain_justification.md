# XR Domain Justification: WebXR + Glasses-Free ELF-SR1 vs. Enclosed HMD VR

Deploying spatial computing outdoors across UNESCO heritage monuments in coastal South India (**Mahabalipuram Shore Temple**, Tamil Nadu) imposes environmental, physiological, and operational constraints where enclosed Head-Mounted Displays (VR/MR headsets) fail.

| Constraint Dimension | Enclosed VR/MR Headset (Rejected Outdoors) | Lunar Aurora Hybrid Architecture (Chosen) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solar Thermal Load ($>35^\circ\text{C}$)** | Facial gasket traps heat and sweat; external cameras overheat and throttle within 10-15 minutes in coastal midday sun. | Mobile WebXR utilizes the tourist's own pocket smartphone in brief gaze/scan bursts; active route engine steers visitors into **76.0% less sun exposure**. |
| **Tourist Hygiene & Throughput** | Shared headsets at public counters require multi-minute sanitization cycles between thousands of daily visitors. | Zero-install QR-launched WebXR on personal devices (`BYOD`) + **zero-wearable Sony ELF-SR1** eye-tracked 4K kiosk eliminates shared facial contact completely. |
| **Situational & Coastal Safety** | Heavy passthrough frames narrow peripheral vision around ancient stone steps, uneven bedrock, and bay swells. | Camera-first WebXR overlay preserves full natural peripheral awareness while walking; **HRTF binaural audio** guides attention hands-free without staring down at screens. |
| **Heritage Museum Counter Showcase** | Putting first-time elderly visitors or children into VR isolates them from family groups. | The **Sony ELF-SR1 Kiosk** renders volumetric 3D Pallava temple layers at the entrance visitor center with glasses-free eye-parallax readable together. |
